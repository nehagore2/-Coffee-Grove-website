OUR ESTATE

A grove is more than rows of coffee.

The coffee plants grow alongside fruit trees, flowering plants and old shade trees. Rain, soil, altitude and patience shape the crop long before roasting begins. Our approach is simple: protect the land, respect the season and let the coffee speak for itself.

We work in small lots so we can follow each harvest closely — from picking and processing to roasting and packing.

THE HARVEST

Picked when
it is ready.

Harvest is a quiet rhythm. Ripe cherries are selected by hand, keeping green and overripe fruit out of the lot. That extra care creates a cleaner, sweeter cup.
